## Generation prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Create an original dreamworld concept artwork for the onboarding of a premium, playful personal AI Mac app. Wide landscape composition approximately 16:10. A serene imaginative landscape of softly sculpted floating mineral terraces above a luminous sea of clouds; a graceful pale opalescent ribbon-like walking path curves across the low terraces and disappears into distant morning haze. A few oversized translucent petal-like sculptural forms catch light near the right edge, like an unfamiliar but welcoming natural world. Gentle, fluid transitions of pale sky blue, mint, butter yellow, peach and dusty lavender; modest saturation with a few carefully placed richer coral accents. Soft sunny daylight, spacious sky, atmospheric depth, softly tactile fine-grained surfaces, delicate pearlescent highlights, beautifully crafted three-dimensional illustration with an editorial art sensibility. Welcoming, curious, magical and sophisticated, with a sense of freedom and possibility. Avoid a sterile architectural render or generic abstract wallpaper: it should feel like a place one could explore. Keep the upper half and central-left area quiet and pale so real onboarding text or a frosted panel could be placed there later; concentrate the most detailed scenery around the lower third and right edge. Maintain real depth and some gentle contrast rather than washing everything into white. This must be a new original composition, not a recreation of any existing brand's illustration. No dogs, mascots, people, recognizable logos, houses with arched openings, waterfalls, lettering, UI panels, buttons, device frames, watermark or typography. Artwork only, edge to edge.
